Flying Pancakes: The Top 100 Pancakes of the World

A reading route through a hundred pancakes chosen for reach and usefulness, not a prize list.

Appalachian Griddle Cakes

Buckwheat, sorghum, dried apple, and corn on an upland griddle.

Across the American South

A train of buttermilk, corn, rice, and the Gulf, from the Piedmont toward New Orleans.

The British Isles & Ireland

Shrove pancakes, oatcakes, pikelets, boxty, and farls.

Crepes of France

Wheat crepes, buckwheat galettes, and a chickpea socca at the last stop.

East Asia's Savory Pancakes

Jianbing, scallion pancakes, okonomiyaki, jeon, and bánh xèo.

India's Dosas & Cheelas

Fermented rice and lentil batters, and the gram-flour cakes cooked the same morning.

Africa Coast to Coast

Fermented batters, plantain, and a cinnamon pannekoek, from the Atlantic to the Horn and south.

Latin America & the Caribbean

Masa, fresh corn, cassava, and the hotcake that arrived later.

Shrove Tuesday Around the World

The late-winter custom of using eggs and fat, with each feast day named honestly.

Breakfast Pancakes

The morning stack, from buttermilk cakes to dosa and plättar.

Savory Pancakes

Scallion, cabbage, cheese, and chile. Cakes that stand in for the meal.

Dessert Pancakes

Sugar, fruit, cheese, and syrup, after the meal or in the middle of a feast.

Potato Cakes

Boxty, latkes, rösti, and the other cakes that start with a potato.

Fermented Batters

Injera, dosa, baghrir, kisra, and the other cakes that sour before they cook.

Plättar, lefse, svele, aebleskiver, and their neighbors.

Molded Batter Cakes

Poffertjes, aebleskiver, khanom khrok, and the other cakes cooked in a dimpled pan.

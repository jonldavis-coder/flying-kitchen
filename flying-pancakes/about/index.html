About

Flying Pancakes of the World gathers pancakes and their griddled relatives. This issue has 290 pancakes. The public goal is 1,000. The count on the site is the number of edited entries, and it will change when new entries are added.

Each stop has a home recipe, a place on the route, a cultural note, a short history, a note on why it tastes this way, an authentic touch, and one to three field notes. If a popular origin story is disputed, the page says so or leaves it out.

Photographs are real pictures downloaded from Wikimedia Commons under CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA, or public domain. Each one carries the photographer and the license. A photograph is used as the dish only when it shows that pancake. When it does not, the page shows a landscape of the place and says so. Book covers and route lines are drawings made for this site. The route map uses Natural Earth country shapes and Leaflet, with no paid map key. Full credits are on the photo credits page. It is a sister site to Flying Sauces of the World.

The community tools follow the same pattern as the related project Granny Recipes: comments, hearts, star ratings, and reader submissions. Twists are extra here. Until Supabase is connected, a banner says that those notes stay in the browser.

The site is published by jonldavis-coder and hosted on GitHub Pages from the main branch.

Peanut chikki

Peanut and jaggery brittle to nibble while flying kites at Uttarayan. A home batch, with a warning about hot sugar.

10 min · about 15 min · one small tray

Tradition: Gujarat’s Uttarayan

Ingredients

  • 150 g roasted unsalted peanuts
  • 120 g jaggery, grated or chopped
  • 1 teaspoon ghee, plus a little for the tray
  • A pinch of cardamom

Steps

  1. Grease a tray or a piece of parchment. Have a spatula ready. The sugar will set quickly.
  2. Warm the jaggery and ghee in a heavy pan over medium heat. Stir until the jaggery melts and a drop in a cup of cold water turns hard and brittle, not soft.
  3. Take the pan off the heat. Stir in the peanuts and cardamom. Scrape the mass onto the tray and press it thin with a greased spatula.
  4. Score squares while it is still warm. Cool completely, then break apart.

Hot jaggery burns. Keep children away from the pan.

Mint and Wikipedia name chikki, peanut or sesame with jaggery, as an Uttarayan snack. This is one home way to make the peanut kind. Sesame can replace the peanuts for a til chikki.

Kite sandwiches

KiteKitchen original. Diamond sandwiches with a bread tail, for a park picnic. Not a festival tradition.

20 min · no cook · 4 sandwiches

Ingredients

  • 8 slices of soft sandwich bread
  • Butter or mayonnaise
  • Fillings that will not slide: sliced cheese and cucumber, or egg salad
  • A little lettuce if you want it

Steps

  1. Butter the bread. Add the filling and close the sandwiches.
  2. Cut each sandwich into a diamond. Keep the offcuts.
  3. From an offcut, cut a long thin strip. Tuck one end under the bottom point of the diamond so it hangs like a kite tail.
  4. Wrap them and eat them at the park. They are a shape, not a historic dish.

KiteKitchen original. Do not describe these as a traditional kite-festival food.

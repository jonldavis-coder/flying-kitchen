Kite sugar cookies

KiteKitchen original. Diamond sugar cookies with icing spars and a small tail. Not a traditional recipe.

30 min, plus chilling · 11 to 13 min · about 16 cookies

Ingredients

  • 180 g plain flour
  • 80 g softened unsalted butter
  • 80 g sugar
  • 1 egg yolk
  • 1/2 teaspoon vanilla
  • A pinch of salt
  • Icing: 100 g icing sugar and 1 tablespoon milk, plus a drop of food coloring if you want it

Steps

  1. Beat the butter and sugar. Beat in the yolk and vanilla. Stir in the flour and salt. Wrap and chill 30 minutes.
  2. Heat the oven to 175°C. Roll the dough about 5 mm thick. Cut diamonds. Bake on a lined tray 11 to 13 minutes, until the edges are just gold. Cool.
  3. Mix the icing until it pipes. Draw a cross of spars on each diamond and a short tail of three dots past the bottom point.

KiteKitchen original. The shape is the joke. There is no old cookbook behind it.

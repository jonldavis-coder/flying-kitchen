Park kite mix

KiteKitchen original. A salty-sweet tin of chickpeas, peanuts, seeds, and dried mango to take to the field.

10 min · 25 min for the chickpeas · about 3 cups

Ingredients

  • 1 can chickpeas, drained, rinsed, and dried
  • 1 teaspoon oil
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 cup roasted peanuts
  • 1/4 cup pumpkin seeds
  • 1/4 cup dried mango, cut in strips

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Toss the chickpeas with the oil and salt. Roast on a tray about 25 minutes, shaking once, until they are crisp at the edge. Cool.
  2. Mix with the peanuts, seeds, and mango. Pack in a tin.
  3. If you have peanut chikki, break a few pieces in. That is optional and it is the Uttarayan brittle, not part of an older trail mix.

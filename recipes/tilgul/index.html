Tilgul laddoos

Sesame and jaggery laddoos for Makar Sankranti, the sweet Maharashtra Tourism and the BBC describe people exchanging.

15 min · about 10 min · 12 small laddoos

Tradition: Maharashtra’s Makar Sankranti

Ingredients

  • 150 g white sesame seeds
  • 80 g jaggery, chopped
  • 1 teaspoon ghee

Steps

  1. Toast the sesame in a dry pan until it smells nutty and a seed or two pops. Tip it into a bowl.
  2. Melt the jaggery with the ghee over low heat until it is loose and glossy. Do not take it to a hard crack. You want it sticky enough to bind.
  3. Stir the sesame back in. When the mixture is cool enough to handle but still warm, press it into small balls. If it crumbles, warm it again for a moment.
  4. Cool on a plate. They keep in a tin for several days.

The greeting Maharashtra Tourism and the BBC record is “tilgul ghya, god god bola”: take the sweet and speak sweetly. The spelling of the Marathi line varies by source. The sense they give is the same.

This is a plain laddoo. Gul poli, the jaggery flatbread Maharashtra Tourism also lists, is a different dish and is not this recipe.

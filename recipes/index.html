From the festivals

Codfish cakes for a kite day

A home version of Bermuda’s Good Friday codfish cake: soaked salt cod, potato, onion, parsley, and thyme, fried. Recipes on the island vary.

Bermuda Good Friday kites

Hot cross buns

A home batch of hot cross buns for Good Friday: an enriched raisin dough, a flour cross, and a light sugar glaze.

Bermuda Good Friday kites

Stovetop undhiyu

A stovetop home undhiyu for Uttarayan: winter vegetables, a coconut-herb paste, and fried fenugreek dumplings. The buried-pot method is described, not reproduced.

Gujarat’s Uttarayan

Peanut chikki

Peanut and jaggery brittle to nibble while flying kites at Uttarayan. A home batch, with a warning about hot sugar.

Gujarat’s Uttarayan

Tilgul laddoos

Sesame and jaggery laddoos for Makar Sankranti, the sweet Maharashtra Tourism and the BBC describe people exchanging.

Maharashtra’s Makar Sankranti

A shortened fiambre

A small cold salad in the spirit of Guatemalan fiambre for 1 November. A family fiambre uses many more ingredients.

Giant kites in Sumpango and Santiago Sacatepéquez

Qingtuan, with a note on the green

Green glutinous rice balls for Qingming. Mugwort or barley grass is the traditional color. Spinach juice is named here only as a stand-in.

Qingming kites and qingtuan

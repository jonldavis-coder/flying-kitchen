Kite hand pies

KiteKitchen original. Diamond jam hand pies with a pastry tail, to carry to a kite field.

25 min · 18 to 22 min · 6 pies

Ingredients

  • One sheet of puff pastry, thawed, or a double batch of shortcrust
  • 6 teaspoons jam or thick apple butter
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • A pinch of sugar

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Cut the pastry into six diamonds and six small strips for tails.
  2. Put a teaspoon of jam in the center of each diamond, off to one side of where the fold will land. Fold the diamond in half into a smaller diamond, or top it with a second diamond if you cut extras. Press the edges with a fork.
  3. Brush with egg, cut one small vent, and press a pastry strip onto the bottom point.
  4. Bake 18 to 22 minutes, until the pastry is deep gold. Cool before you pack them. Jam stays hot.

KiteKitchen original. Use a thick jam so it does not run out in the oven.

About Kite Kitchen

Kite Kitchen means flying around the world and tasting different foods.

Flying around the world, one simple recipe at a time. Three guides keep their own names here: Flying Sauces, Flying Pancakes, and Flying BBQ. A recipe stays with the guide and the place it was already filed under.

The crest is the gold kite in its frame. This page shows the full crest. The header uses the kite with the wordmark.

The public address is kitekitchen.com. This site does not add reviews or star ratings. The Kite Kitchen shop is coming soon.

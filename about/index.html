About the hub

A journal for three Flying sites

Flying Kitchen is the flagship shelf for Flying Sauces, Flying Pancakes, and Flying BBQ. The voice is a travel journal: hot air balloon, plane, train, and boat. The work is recipes and where they come from.

At build time the hub reads each site’s public repository. Flying Sauces is in this issue with 278 sauces. Flying Pancakes is in this issue with 290 pancakes. Flying BBQ shows a coming-soon section until it publishes a catalog. The next rebuild fills a coming-soon section from that catalog.

Accuracy

Counts, journeys, books, and settlement lines come from those sites. Featured recipes are stops on published journeys. The Top 100 is the sister site’s own book, when one exists. Editor’s picks are that site’s flagship books.

Dishes, origins, and book lists come from the sister sites’ published pages. Community ratings start empty. A comment appears only after a reader posts one in demo mode, and it stays in that browser.

The shop

Flying Kitchen Gear is a plan: aprons, grill tools, cutting boards, cast-iron skillets, spice-rub tins, meat thermometers, grill gloves, branding irons, tumblers, and hats, each drawn with the logo. Sourcing is planned as print-on-demand or private label. Prices live in the shop config as placeholders. The Ultimate Flying Kitchen Library is every published Flying book, at 30% off the combined placeholder price.

Food

Recipes on the sister sites are for home cooking. They are not professional food-safety or medical advice.

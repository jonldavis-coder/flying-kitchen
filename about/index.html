About Kite Kitchen

The name Kite Kitchen means flying around the world and tasting different foods. Flying around the world, one simple recipe at a time.

Jonathan Davis publishes three guides here, under their own names: Flying Sauces, Flying Pancakes, and Flying BBQ. The world map is a way to browse those recipes by country.

The public address is kitekitchen.com. That origin is the SITE_ORIGIN value in site.config.mjs.

Counts are the number of entries imported from those guides. This site does not add reviews or star ratings.

hello@kitekitchen.com is the contact address written into the pages. Jonathan still needs to confirm the mailbox.

Jersey Shore Grilled Bluefish

Rich, oily bluefish with lemon, salt, and a grilled edge.

History

Atlantic bluefish is a seasonal catch off the Jersey Shore and is best grilled the day it is landed or bought, because the oil turns quickly. Beach towns grill it in summer when the boats are running. It is a fish plate, not a smoked mullet and not a chowder.

Cultural background

Fresh bluefish on a hot grill. Buy it fresh and cook it soon. It is an oily fish with a strong flavor when it is old.

Why it tastes this way

Bluefish oil takes a little char and lemon better than a lean white fillet does, and the flavor is fuller. Salt and a hot grate are enough. Stale bluefish tastes muddy, which is a fat problem, not a sauce problem.

How it is made

Oil, salt, and grill bluefish fillets or steaks until they flake.

Authentic touch

Cook it the day you buy it. If it smells overly strong before it hits the grill, do not try to hide it.

How it is used

Off the skin if you grilled skin-on, with lemon. A tomato salad fits beside it.

Field notes

  • Bluefish is an oily Atlantic fish, and Jersey Shore cooks treat freshness as part of the recipe.
  • A hot grill browns the skin quickly, so the fillet needs only a short cook to reach temperature.

Ingredients

  • 4 bluefish fillets, about 6 ounces each, skin on
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon kosher salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 lemon

Steps

  1. Pat the fillets dry. Oil, salt, and pepper them. Cook them the day you bought them.
  2. Heat a grill until the grate is hot and oil it.
  3. Grill skin side down, then turn once, until the thickest part reaches 145°F (63°C) and the fish flakes and looks opaque.
  4. Serve with lemon. Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours.

Cook fish to 145°F (63°C), until it is opaque and flakes.

Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours and use within 3 to 4 days, or freeze. Reheat leftovers to 165°F (74°C). This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • Steaks instead of fillets work if they are about an inch thick. Use the thermometer either way.

Pairings

  • lemon
  • tomato salad
  • boiled potatoes

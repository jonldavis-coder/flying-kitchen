Pumpkin Pie

A pumpkin custard in a pastry crust, baked until the center is set.

  • 20 min prep
  • 55 min cook
  • Serves 8
  • 8 slices

8 servings

History

Pumpkin puree, eggs, evaporated milk, and spice, baked in a pastry crust. Use plain pumpkin puree. Pumpkin pie filling already contains sugar and spice, and it is a different product. No harvest figure is added on this page.

Cultural background

Pumpkin pie is the dessert on the Thanksgiving table on this site.

Why it tastes this way

The eggs and evaporated milk set into a custard. Cinnamon, ginger, and cloves are the spice. Sugar sweetens the puree, which is not sweet on its own.

How it is made

Whisk the puree, sugar, spice, eggs, and evaporated milk, pour the filling into an unbaked crust, and bake until the center is just set.

Authentic touch

Stop when the center still quivers. A filling that is baked until it is rigid often cracks as it cools.

How it is used

Sliced after the roast. It is a dessert, not a side.

Field notes

  • The filling uses pumpkin puree. A can labeled pumpkin pie filling already has sugar and spice in it.
  • The eggs are baked in the custard until the center is set.

Advertisement

Ingredients

  • 1 unbaked 9-inch pie crust
  • 15 ounces pumpkin puree, not pumpkin pie filling
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground ginger
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground cloves
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 1/2 cups evaporated milk

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 375°F (190°C). Fit the pie crust into a 9-inch pie plate.
  2. Whisk the pumpkin puree with the sugar, cinnamon, ginger, cloves, and salt. Whisk in the eggs and the evaporated milk until the filling is smooth.
  3. Pour the filling into the crust.
  4. Bake until the edge is set and the center still quivers slightly, about 50 to 60 minutes. A knife inserted near the center should come out clean.
  5. Cool the pie before slicing.

Bake the custard until the center is set so the eggs are cooked. Cool it, then refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours.

Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours and use within 3 to 4 days, or freeze. Reheat leftovers to 165°F (74°C). This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Pairings

  • Roast turkey
  • Bread stuffing

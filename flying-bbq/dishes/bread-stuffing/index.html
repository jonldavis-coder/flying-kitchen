Bread Stuffing

Bread cubes baked with onion, celery, and sage, in a dish rather than inside the turkey.

  • 20 min prep
  • 50 min cook
  • Serves 8
  • about 8 servings

History

Bread cubes baked with onion, celery, sage, and thyme. This version goes in a dish, not into the turkey. The page does not date the dish.

Cultural background

This is the bread stuffing for the Thanksgiving table on this site. Sage, onion, and celery are the seasoning.

Why it tastes this way

Butter and broth moisten the bread. Sage and thyme are the herbs. Baking it uncovered at the end browns the top without drying the center.

How it is made

Cook the onion and celery in butter, toss them with bread and herbs, moisten with broth, and bake in a covered dish, then uncovered.

Authentic touch

Check the center of the dish with a thermometer. Browning on top is not the same as a hot center.

How it is used

Served beside roast turkey. It is not packed into the bird.

Field notes

  • USDA recommends baking stuffing in a casserole rather than inside the turkey.
  • The center of stuffing still has to reach 165°F (74°C), in a dish or in a bird.

Ingredients

  • 10 cups day-old white bread cubes, about 1 pound
  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter
  • 1 large onion , chopped
  • 3 celery stalks, chopped
  • 2 teaspoons dried sage
  • 1 teaspoon dried thyme
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 1/2 cups turkey or chicken broth

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F (180°C). Butter a baking dish.
  2. Melt the butter in a wide pan. Cook the onion and the celery until they are soft. Take the pan off the heat.
  3. Toss the bread cubes with the sage, thyme, salt, pepper, and the cooked onion and celery. Mix in the broth until the bread is moist, not soupy. Mix the wet and dry ingredients just before baking.
  4. Spread the stuffing in the dish. Cover it and bake 30 minutes. Uncover it and bake about 15 minutes more, until the top is browned and the center reaches 165°F (74°C).

The center of the stuffing must reach 165°F (74°C). This recipe bakes it in a dish, not inside the turkey.

Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours and use within 3 to 4 days, or freeze. Reheat leftovers to 165°F (74°C). This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Pairings

  • Roast turkey
  • Cranberry sauce
  • Bread sauce

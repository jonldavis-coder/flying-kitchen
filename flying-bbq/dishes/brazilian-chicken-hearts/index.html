Brazilian Chicken Heart Skewers

Salted chicken hearts with a browned outside and a fully cooked center.

History

Chicken hearts, coração, are a standard skewer at Brazilian churrascarias. They are poultry offal, salted and grilled as everyday food. Because they are poultry, this page cooks them to 165°F (74°C), the ground-poultry temperature used here for hearts. They are everyday protein on the fire, often one of the first skewers to come off.

Cultural background

Trim the veins and any hard bits, salt the hearts, and thread them so they cook evenly. They are not beef heart, which is the Peruvian anticucho on another page, and they are not a chicken sausage.

Why it tastes this way

Hearts are lean and mineral, and coarse salt plus a hot skewer give them a crust before they dry out. Charcoal is the usual fire. They taste direct and savory, which is why they sit on the same rod as the larger cuts without a sweet glaze.

How it is made

Salt chicken hearts and grill them on skewers to 165°F (74°C).

Authentic touch

Thread the hearts close together so they stay juicy, and spread the skewers so the heat still reaches the centers.

How it is used

Eat them from the skewer with a vinaigrette relish and farofa.

Field notes

  • Chicken hearts are a standard skewer at Brazilian churrascarias and are called coração.
  • They are poultry, and this recipe cooks them to 165°F (74°C).

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 pounds chicken hearts, trimmed
  • 1 teaspoon coarse salt
  • 1 tablespoon neutral oil
  • Metal skewers

Steps

  1. Trim the hearts, dry them, and toss them with oil and salt. They are raw poultry and are not ready to eat. Thread them on skewers. These are poultry and are not ready to eat until they reach 165°F (74°C).
  2. Heat a grill until it is hot. These are poultry and are not ready to eat until they reach 165°F (74°C).
  3. Grill, turning, until the center of a heart in the middle of the skewer reads 165°F (74°C).
  4. Every heart you serve should have reached 165°F (74°C). Rest them a minute and eat them hot. Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours.

Cook ground poultry and sausages made from it to 165°F (74°C).

Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours and use within 3 to 4 days, or freeze. Reheat leftovers to 165°F (74°C). This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • A squeeze of lime at the table is enough. A heavy marinade is optional and not required.
  • Beef heart is a different skewer, with a different temperature, on the Peruvian page.

Pairings

  • molho a campanha
  • farofa
  • lime

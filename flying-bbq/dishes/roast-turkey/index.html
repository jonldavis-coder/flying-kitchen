Roast Turkey

A whole turkey roasted in a home oven and cooked to 165°F (74°C).

  • 25 min prep
  • 3 hr cook
  • Serves 10

History

A whole turkey roasted in a home oven, with salt, pepper, and oil. The bird is left unstuffed. This is a different recipe from the hickory-smoked breast. No origin story is added on this page.

Cultural background

Roast turkey is a common centerpiece on American Thanksgiving tables. Cooking it unstuffed lets the breast and the thigh be checked on their own.

Why it tastes this way

Salt seasons the skin. A 325°F (165°C) oven is the moderate setting USDA uses for roasting a whole turkey. The thermometer decides when it is done, not the clock.

How it is made

Dry the turkey, season it, and roast it uncovered until the thickest part of the breast and the innermost thigh both reach 165°F (74°C).

Authentic touch

Measure both the breast and the thigh, not touching bone. If the turkey has a pop-up timer, check with a thermometer anyway.

How it is used

Carved as the centerpiece. The stuffing on this site is baked in its own dish.

Field notes

  • USDA’s oven for a whole turkey is no lower than 325°F (165°C).
  • USDA’s timetable for an unstuffed 12- to 14-pound turkey at that temperature is about 3 to 3 3/4 hours. The thermometer overrides the timetable.

Ingredients

  • 1 whole turkey, about 12 pounds, neck and giblets removed
  • 2 tablespoons kosher salt
  • 1 teaspoon black pepper
  • 2 tablespoons neutral oil
  • 1 onion , quartered

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 325°F (165°C). Take the neck and giblets out of the turkey and pat the turkey dry. Rub the skin with the oil, salt, and pepper.
  2. Put the onion in the cavity. Set the turkey on a rack in a roasting pan.
  3. Roast it uncovered. An unstuffed turkey of about 12 pounds often needs about 3 hours at this temperature. Start checking before then.
  4. The turkey is done when a thermometer in the thickest part of the breast and in the innermost part of the thigh, not touching bone, reads 165°F (74°C) in both places.
  5. Let the turkey stand 20 minutes, then carve it.

Cook poultry to 165°F (74°C) in the thickest part of the breast and in the innermost thigh, not touching bone.

Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours and use within 3 to 4 days, or freeze. Reheat leftovers to 165°F (74°C). This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Africa's fires by plane

Mechoui, dibi, suya, nyama choma, and a braai.

Africa does not have one grill. This flight is a way to taste how far the fires sit from each other: a Moroccan celebration lamb, a Dakar street dibi, Hausa suya, a Ghanaian tilapia, Kenyan nyama choma, Mozambican peri-peri chicken, and a South African braai sausage.

The plane is honest about the distance. It is not a claim that one coast taught the others.

Stops

  1. Moroccan Mechoui · Morocco — Lamb with cumin, salt, and the fat of a long roast.
  2. Senegalese Dibi · Senegal — Grilled lamb with onion and a sharp mustard relish.
  3. Nigerian Suya · Nigeria — Beef with a hot peanut spice, smoke, and a salty crust.
  4. Ghanaian Grilled Tilapia · Ghana — Charred tilapia with ginger, garlic, and chili.
  5. Kenyan Nyama Choma · Kenya — Goat with salt, a charred edge, and a squeeze of lime or chili salt.
  6. Mozambican Peri-Peri Chicken · Mozambique — Charcoal chicken with garlic, lemon, and a very hot peri-peri chile.
  7. South African Boerewors · South Africa — Beef and pork sausage with toasted coriander, vinegar, and a grilled casing.

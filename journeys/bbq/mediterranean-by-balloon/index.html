Mediterranean by balloon

A low pass over sardines, calçots, vine shoots, and a spit.

A balloon is the right speed for this coast. The fire changes with the harbor: sardines on a Malaga beach, spring onions in Catalonia, lamb chops over Rioja vine cuttings, June sardines in Lisbon, an Easter lamb in Greece, a hand-minced kebab in Adana.

Nothing here is a fast transfer. The point is how close these shores are, and how different the grills still are.

Stops

  1. Malaga Sardine Skewers · Spain — Charred sardines, salt, olive oil, and smoke from a beach fire.
  2. Catalan Calcots · Spain — Sweet grilled spring onions with a blackened jacket and a soft, juicy center.
  3. Rioja Vine-Shoot Lamb Chops · Spain — Small lamb chops with a fast char and the scent of burning vine wood.
  4. Lisbon June Sardines · Portugal — Charred sardines, coarse salt, and olive oil, eaten with bread.
  5. Greek Easter Spit Lamb · Greece — Lamb with oregano, lemon, garlic, and wood smoke from a long turn on the spit.
  6. Adana Kebab · Turkey — Hand-minced lamb, hot pepper, and tail fat, smoky from a close charcoal fire.

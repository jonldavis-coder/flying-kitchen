Flying Pancakes · Boat

Scandinavia by boat

Lefse, svele, plättar, and aebleskiver, harbor to harbor.

These cakes already knew the water. A boat is how rye, butter, and later cloudberries moved, and how the recipes later crossed to the Upper Midwest.

The American church-basement versions are mentioned on the pages. The recipes here are the Scandinavian ones.

Stops

  1. Norwegian lefse · Norway — Potato lefse, rolled thin and griddled, soft enough to fold with butter and sugar.
  2. Svele · Norway — Thick west-Norwegian pancakes, the ferry kiosk cake, with jam or brown whey cheese.
  3. Swedish pannkakor · Sweden — Thin Swedish pancakes, pan-cooked, served with lingonberry jam.
  4. Swedish plättar · Sweden — Tiny Swedish pancakes from a plätt pan, with jam and cream.
  5. Danish pandekager · Denmark — Thin Danish pancakes, rolled with sugar or jam.
  6. Aebleskiver · Denmark — Round Danish pancakes from a molded pan, cardamom, jam, and powdered sugar.
  7. Finnish lettu · Finland — Thin Finnish pancakes, served with jam, sugar, or whipped cream.
  8. Icelandic pönnukökur · Iceland — Very thin Icelandic pancakes, rolled with sugar or rhubarb jam.

Caribbean by boat

Island to island: sour orange, allspice, pepper, and herb pastes.

Caribbean sauces traveled by sea. Sour orange and garlic, scotch bonnet, vinegar pickles, and herb pastes are dockside food.

This leg is by boat, the way these flavors actually moved between islands.

Open this route on Flying Sauces See it on the map

Stops

  1. Mojo Criollo · Cuba — Sour orange, garlic, cumin, and oil: sharp, garlicky, and savory.
  2. Jerk Marinade · Jamaica — Scotch bonnet, allspice, thyme, and scallion: hot, aromatic, and smoky-sweet.
  3. Haitian Epis · Haiti — Bell pepper, garlic, thyme, and scallion: savory and herbal.
  4. Puerto Rican Sofrito · Puerto Rico — Ají dulce, pepper, cilantro, and culantro: sweet, herbal, and savory.
  5. Trinidadian Pepper Sauce · Trinidad and Tobago — Scotch bonnet or Congo pepper, vinegar, and garlic: very hot and sharp.
  6. Sauce Chien · France — Lime, scallion, garlic, chile, and oil: sharp, herbal, and hot.
  7. Wasakaka · Dominican Republic — Lime, garlic, and oregano: sharp, herbal, and savory.

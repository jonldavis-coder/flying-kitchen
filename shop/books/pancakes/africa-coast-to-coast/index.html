Africa Coast to Coast

Fermented batters, plantain, and a cinnamon pannekoek, from the Atlantic to the Horn and south.

This is a reading route, not a claim that one batter explains the continent. Baghrir, masa, injera, and pannekoek do not come from one kitchen.

The plane is the vehicle because the distance is real. A boat would be the right machine for a single coast, and the wrong one for this book.

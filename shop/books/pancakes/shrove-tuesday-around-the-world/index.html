Shrove Tuesday Around the World

The late-winter custom of using eggs and fat, with each feast day named honestly.

Shrove Tuesday is the English and Irish day. It is not the name of every pancake holiday.

French crepes on this route belong to Chandeleur, 2 February. Russian blini belong to Maslenitsa, butter week. Galician filloas belong to Carnival. The pages say which day is which. The book is a route through that season, not a claim that the whole world keeps an English Tuesday.

Privacy

This is a plain-language notice, not a lawyer’s privacy policy.

The pages are static files. A recipe does not ask you to make an account. The notify buttons open your own email app and send nothing to a KiteKitchen server, because there isn’t one.

Google Analytics

KiteKitchen uses Google Analytics 4 with measurement ID G-JHDKNY6NTV. The Analytics library is not requested until you press, type, or scroll, or until a short wait after the page loads, so it does not sit on the first paint.

Before that library loads, the page sets Google Consent Mode defaults. In the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland, analytics cookies start denied and stay denied until you accept. Elsewhere, analytics storage starts granted unless you reject. If you reject, a later visit keeps analytics storage denied. If you accept, it is granted. The choice is stored in this browser under the key kk-consent.

When storage is denied, Google’s tag can still receive a cookieless signal. That is how Consent Mode is built. Advertising storage stays denied until you accept, and the ad script is not loaded at all while ads are switched off.

Advertising

Ad slots are reserved and labeled. They show a KiteKitchen shop note while AdSense is disabled. No publisher ID is in the repository. If AdSense is turned on later, its script loads only after you accept. Google requires a certified consent platform for personalized ads in the EEA and the UK. This banner is not a certified platform.

Affiliates

There are no affiliate links. If some are added later, the merchant, such as Amazon, may set a cookie when you follow one. The affiliate disclosure will apply.

Flying Sauces and Flying Pancakes pages can still store their own demo notes in this browser. Those notes are not a KiteKitchen account.

Bermuda Good Friday kites

Good Friday in Bermuda is a kite day. Families build tissue-paper kites and, alongside them, eat codfish cakes and hot cross buns. The pairing is described by Bermudian sources as part of the day, not as an ancient single recipe with one official formula.

Gujarat’s Uttarayan

In Gujarat, Makar Sankranti is Uttarayan, a public kite festival around 14 January. The foods repeatedly named with it are undhiyu, chikki, and jalebi.

Maharashtra’s Makar Sankranti

Maharashtra Tourism describes Makar Sankranti, on 14 January, as a harvest festival of sesame-and-jaggery sweets and kite flying. The sentence people say while sharing the sweet is the part the sources repeat.

Giant kites in Sumpango and Santiago Sacatepéquez

On the Day of the Saints, 1 November, communities in Santiago Sacatepéquez and Sumpango build and exhibit giant tissue-paper kites. Fiambre, a cold mixed salad, is the dish Guatemalan sources tie to that same day.

Qingming kites and qingtuan

Qingming, the Pure Brightness festival around 4 or 5 April, is a day for tending graves and then going outside. Smithsonian and the Shanghai government both name kite flying among the outings, and qingtuan as a food of the season in the lower Yangtze.

Toyomansi

Soy and calamansi: salty, bright, and thin.

History

Toyomansi is the Filipino dipping sauce of toyo, soy sauce, and kalamansi, the local citrus. Chile and garlic are often added. It is the sawsawan for grilled pork, lumpia, and fish. Lime can stand in for calamansi and will taste a little different.

Cultural background

A grilled-meat table in the Philippines is expected to have this dip. It is thin on purpose so it seasons each bite.

Why it tastes this way

Soy is salty and calamansi or lime is bright. Together they taste thin and sharp, which is right for a dip. A glaze would be reduced and sweet. This is not reduced.

How it is made

Soy sauce and calamansi or lime are stirred with garlic and chile.

Authentic touch

Use fresh citrus. Stir and dip. Do not boil it down.

How it is used

Serve it as a dip for grilled pork or lumpia.

Field notes

  • Toyo is soy and mansi is calamansi. The name is the recipe.
  • It is a dip, not a glaze you reduce.

Advertisement

Ingredients

  • 1/4 cup soy sauce
  • 2 tablespoons calamansi or lime juice
  • 1 garlic clove, minced
  • 1 bird’s eye chile , sliced

Steps

  1. Stir all the ingredients.
  2. Taste for citrus.
  3. Serve the same day.
  4. Dip grilled meat into it.

Refrigerate and use within a few days unless the recipe gives a different time. This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • Vinegar instead of citrus is a different sawsawan.
  • A sliced chile is the usual heat.

Pairings

  • inasal
  • lumpia
  • grilled fish
